CognitiveTwinXR Equipment + Tabletop v10

Deploy: extract this ZIP and upload its contents together. index.html must be
at the deployment root. Keep vendor/, model.json and all supporting files.
Use HTTPS and open index.html. Hard-refresh after replacing an older version.

Browser: choose Room equipment. Click a device model to open its settings.
Adjust its 0-10 level or turn it on/off. Readings and effects are simulations.

Tabletop AR: Room 110 opens automatically with ghosted building surfaces.
Point at a device and pinch (or use a controller trigger) to open Devices.
The selected device has a dot. Its main card toggles power; +/- adjusts level.
The left-palm MENU also opens the Devices and Model tabs.

Point at the building and hold a pinch with one hand to move it; turn your
hand to rotate it. Add a second held pinch aimed at the building, then spread
or bring hands together to scale; turn the line between hands to rotate.
Release to stop. Controller grips use the same manipulation system.
Model tab buttons and controller sticks remain available as alternatives.

Checks: equipment state tests, tabletop panel tests, direct device ray
selection, one-hand translation/rotation, two-hand scaling/rotation, and
session restoration passed. Build completed. No physical headset test was
available; browser visual testing was unavailable in this environment.
